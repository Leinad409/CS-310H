**Features in the Project:**

- Loaded the FPS Template and its starter assets in a new project.
  
- Created spinning objects that rotate at different speeds. 
  - Spinning objects- Rock, Arrows, Unreal Ball.
  
- Prints Hello when you press "H" on your keyboard.
  
- If you collide with the barrel, it lights on fire (EX: shoot the barrel with the rifle on the top floor).
  
- Created a platform that has an explosion effect. When this platform is walked over it turns on spark effects over the spinning unity balls.
  
- Created pickups that print a string of the current total of pickup objects to track "points" in a game.
  
- There is a light switch on the floor right before you enter the dark hallway on the left when you enter the building.
  - Light switch turns on and off when the player passes through the object.
